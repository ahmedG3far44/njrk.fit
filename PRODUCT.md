# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Health-conscious individuals who want structured diet plans, training programs, and progress tracking. They use the app to create personalized meal and workout plans based on their profile (goals, dietary restrictions, equipment, religion), log daily progress, and stay motivated through streaks and community. Their context is daily check-ins — morning meal planning, workout logging, evening progress review.

## Product Purpose

Njerka is a holistic health dashboard that generates AI-powered meal plans and workout routines, tracks body metrics over time, and fosters accountability through streaks, leaderboards, and a community feed. Success means users build sustainable habits without needing a personal trainer or nutritionist.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clean, trustworthy. The voice is supportive but not pushy — like an experienced coach who lets results speak. No hype, no guilt, no noisy gamification. The interface should feel like a well-organized training journal: structured, legible, and quietly encouraging.

## Anti-references

- Cold clinical apps (white-on-white, sterile, hospital-clean with no warmth)
- Noisy gamified fitness trackers (constant badges, popups, confetti, leaderboard spam)
- Dark-mode gym bro aesthetics (black/orange, aggressive typography, heavy dropshadows)
- Generic SaaS dashboards (blue nav sidebar, gray cards, no visual hierarchy)

## Design Principles

1. **Calm confidence** — The UI never shouts. Information is findable, not pushed. Feedback is clear but quiet (toast, not modal).
2. **Clarity over density** — Every screen has one focal task. Whitespace is a feature, not waste. Data is surfaced progressively.
3. **Trust through consistency** — Predictable navigation patterns, consistent form controls, no unexpected layout shifts. The app behaves like a well-trained assistant.
4. **Progress as narrative** — Metrics are framed as stories (trends, streaks, adherence rates), not raw numbers in tables. Visualize, don't tabulate.
5. **Ground in the physical** — The subject is food, movement, and the body. Colors, textures, and spacing reference natural materials and real environments, not abstract data worlds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Support for reduced motion. High contrast between text and backgrounds. Form labels associated with inputs. Error messages in plain language.
